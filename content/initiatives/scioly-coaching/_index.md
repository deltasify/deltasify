    - title: "What is Science Olympiad?"
      content: |
        Science Olympiad is a track-meet style academic competition that is open-ended. You will be given "learning goals" and have to design a curriculum to help middle schoolers achieve those goals. 
        
        Volunteers coach 2-4 middle schoolers for Science Olympiad. **We do not require previous experience with teaching or competing in Science Olympiad.**
        
        **There are practice tests and online resources to help you.** You can find out more on the [Official Science Olympiad Website](https://www.soinc.org/events/2027-division-b-events).

The Science Olympiad Coaching Program (SOCP) connects volunteers with middle schoolers to prepare them for Science Olympiad, a track-meet style academic competition. As a coach, you will dedicate approximately one hour per week to develop lecture materials and practice tests, alongside a one-hour weekly coaching session. Having prior Science Olympiad experience is preferred, but not a strict requirement. **You have the flexibility to coach an event independently or team up with a partner.**
